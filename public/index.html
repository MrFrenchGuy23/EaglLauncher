Documentation

Getting Started with EaglLauncher

Welcome to EaglLauncher, the ultimate browser-based Eaglercraft client launcher. No downloads required — play Minecraft directly in your browser.

System Requirements

  • A modern web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Edge, or Safari)
  • Stable internet connection
  • For WASM clients: Chrome 57+ or Edge 79+ recommended
  • For experimental ported clients (1.16+): Chrome with WASM-GC support

How to Play

  1. Choose a Client — Go to the Clients tab and pick one that suits your needs.
  2. Launch the Client — Click the "Play" button. The client opens in a new browser tab.
  3. Connect to a Server — In the game menu, click Multiplayer → Direct Connect.
  4. Enter Server Address — Paste the server's WebSocket address (e.g., wss://example.com).
  5. Join & Play! — Click Join Server and enjoy.

Quick Play

Use the Quick Play tab to select a client and server combination, then hit the big green button to launch instantly.

Tip: Favorites are saved automatically. Star your most-used clients and servers for quick access.

Clients Guide

EaglLauncher includes every Eaglercraft client ever made — over 57 clients across all Minecraft versions.

Client Categories

  • Official — Made by lax1dude, the Eaglercraft creator. Most stable and reliable.
  • Custom — Community-built clients with unique features, UIs, and enhancements.
  • Legacy — Older 1.5.2 clients from the early days of Eaglercraft.
  • Classic — Minecraft Alpha, Beta, Indev, and other retro versions.

Client Types

  • JavaScript (JS) — Works in all browsers. Standard builds.
  • WebAssembly (WASM) — Compiled to WebAssembly for up to 50% better performance. Chrome/Edge only.

Notable Clients

  • EaglercraftX — The flagship client. Supports 1.5.2, 1.8.8, 1.12.2, and even experimental 1.20.
  • Resent Client — Feature-rich competitive client with 5.0 and 5.1 versions.
  • PixelClient — Low-end friendly with clean UI.
  • Astra Client — High-performance with FPS optimizations.
  • DragonX — PvP-focused with multiple iterations (V2 through V5).
  • EaglerForge — Supports Forge mods in the browser.
  • TuffClient — Modern 1.21-style look built on 1.12.2.

Adding Custom Clients

Click "Add Client" in the Clients tab to add your own custom Eaglercraft client URL.

How Servers Work

All Eaglercraft servers use WebSocket connections (wss://). In the game, go to Multiplayer → Direct Connect and paste the server address.

Adding Custom Servers

Click "Add Server" in the Servers tab to add your own server. You need the WebSocket address (wss:// or ws://).

Frequently Asked Questions

Is EaglLauncher free?

Yes! EaglLauncher is 100% free and open source. All clients and servers are accessed directly through the browser.

Do I need to download anything?

No. Everything runs in your browser. Pick a client, click Play, and it opens in a new tab.

What's the difference between JS and WASM?

JS (JavaScript) works in all browsers. WASM (WebAssembly) is faster (up to 50%) but only works in Chrome and Edge.

How do I connect to a server?

In the game, click Multiplayer → Direct Connect → paste the server's wss:// address → Join Server.

Can I add my own clients or servers?

Yes! Use the "Add Client" and "Add Server" buttons in the respective tabs.

Are ported versions stable?

1.12.2 and 1.14 ports are stable. 1.16.5+ ports are experimental and may have bugs or performance issues.

Does this work on mobile?

EaglLauncher works on mobile browsers, but Eaglercraft itself needs a keyboard. Some clients support touch controls.
